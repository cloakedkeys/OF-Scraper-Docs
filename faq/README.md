# FAQ/Common Issues

Common problems and quick answers, grouped by topic:

* [Login & Auth](login-and-auth.md): auth errors, "Double check auth info", "No accounts found", status down, and 403s after a VPN or IP change.
* [Missing Posts & Models](missing-posts-and-models.md): missing posts or messages, model count mismatches, locked content, and `--scrape-paid`.
* [Downloads & Files](downloads-and-files.md): leftover `tempvideo`/`tempaudio` files, false "failed" counts, filename collisions, recurring skips, and network drives.
* [Install, FFmpeg & CDM](install-ffmpeg-and-cdm.md): `ofscraper` not found, FFmpeg paths and shared-library errors, CDM issues, and old 3.14.7 update prompts.
* [HTTP Errors & Fansly](http-errors-and-fansly.md): 404, 409, and 429 responses, slow requests, collecting debug logs, and Fansly issues.
